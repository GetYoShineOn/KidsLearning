# First customer playbook (human-executed)
Goal: 1 paying HVAC/plumbing shop (4-25 employees, 0-2 CSRs, on Housecall Pro/Jobber), then $1,000 cumulative.
1. Build a list of 50 local shops from public sources you are allowed to use (Google Maps, state license boards, BBB). Add to /admin > Prospects (business phone/website only; contact email only if published for business contact).
2. Click Audit on each; sort by score. Read the top 20 audits yourself; delete any finding you would not stand behind.
3. Draft email, edit for voice, set COMPANY_POSTAL_ADDRESS + SENDER_NAME, Approve, send from your own mailbox, click "I sent this". Send at most ~10/day; honor any "no thanks" immediately (DNC button).
4. Warm channels in parallel: people you know in trades, local contractor Facebook/Reddit communities (help, don't pitch), PPC/LSA agencies who could resell.
5. Phone follow-up to business lines is allowed for B2B but check DNC rules and state law first.
6. On interest: offer the 14-day pilot (read-only audit + text-back on their missed calls, month-to-month after, no revenue guarantee). Register 10DLC before real texting; until then run the simulator on a call, not on customers.
7. Record payment in /admin the day it arrives. Track: audits reviewed, emails approved/sent, replies, calls, pilots, paid.
Requires from you: Twilio account + A2P registration, Stripe account, domain + name decision, postal address, attorney review of pilot terms/consent language.
