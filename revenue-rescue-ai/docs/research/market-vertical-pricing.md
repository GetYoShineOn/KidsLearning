**Recommendation.** BEST: residential HVAC (high tickets, high LSA/CPL spend, phone-driven, emergency calls, ServiceTitan/Housecall Pro dominance gives integration paths; seasonality is a risk: summer and winter peaks are when missed calls hurt most, so pilots in shoulder seasons show weaker volume). SECOND: plumbing (very high call urgency, fast sales cycle, less seasonal, lower ticket but high call volume). Roofing has the biggest ticket and the strongest estimate-follow-up story, so it is the best third vertical for the estimate-follow-up workflow later, but storm-driven demand and door-knock/canvass-based sales make speed-to-lead less central.

**Ideal customer profile (ESTIMATE).** Residential HVAC or plumbing, 4-25 employees (3-15 techs), $1M-$8M revenue, 0-2 CSRs (so calls actually go missed; over 2 CSRs and $3M+ moves into Avoca territory), spending $2k-$15k/month on LSA/Angi/PPC, using Housecall Pro, Jobber or ServiceTitan, owner still answers phone or approves marketing spend. Rationale: Avoca's own referral criteria (>$3M revenue, 2+ CSRs, ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro) show the enterprise-priced tier ([FieldCamp](https://fieldcamp.ai/reviews/avoca-ai/), LOW-MED); below that is a price-sensitive gap.

## 2. Most painful leak and first workflow

Ranking for the first build (my reasoning, evidence LOW-MED):

1. **Missed-call text-back plus AI qualification and booking (build first).** Highest measurable pain (paid-for calls with no answer), fastest to prove in a 14-day pilot, needs only call-forwarding/tracking data. Caveat: text-back tools are commoditized at $20-$300/month ([Sonant list](https://www.sonant.ai/blog/missed-call-text-back), [Aira](https://www.getaira.io/blog/missed-call-text-back)), so the differentiator must be the audit and recovery reporting, not the auto-text itself.
2. **Web/LSA/Angi lead speed-to-response.** Shared-lead platforms make first response decisive (complaint pattern in [LeadTruffle](https://www.leadtruffle.co/blog/complete-guide-angi-leads-home-service-contractors-2026/) and others, LOW-MED). Same infrastructure as #1; add second.
3. **Unsold-estimate follow-up.** Largest dollar pool per lead (estimates are 30-40% closed; roughly half of non-buyers buy within a year, LOW confidence), but needs CRM/estimate data access, so build after integrations.
4. **Dormant lead reactivation.** Cheap to sell as a one-off audit-based campaign, highest compliance risk (stale consent, see Section 5). Offer only to customers with documented consent/existing relationship.

Note: the "38% booked" and "27% missed" numbers together imply recoverable revenue is real but smaller than vendor headlines (62% missed x 85% never call back). The audit model should use conservative inputs (Section 7).

## 4. Pricing hypothesis (ESTIMATE; test in pilots)

Anchors: Sameday $449-$789; Smith.ai AI $95-$800; GHL agencies $297-$2,500; Avoca $1,000-$3,000; average booked-job value $350+ blended, HVAC after-hours about $1,400 (vendor-cited, [AInora](https://ainora.lt/blog/hvac-service-call-statistics-2026), LOW).

| Tier | Setup | Monthly | Performance component | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter (owner-operators, 1-3 techs) | $0-$199 | $199 | None | MCTB, 1 number, weekly report, 10DLC registration help |
| Growth (core ICP) | $497 | $599 | Optional 5% of documented recovered job revenue (CRM-verified) above baseline, capped at $500/mo | + AI qualification/booking, web-lead speed, CRM write-back |
| Pro (multi-line, 10-25 staff) | $997 | $1,199 | 5-8% of verified recovered revenue with cap | + estimate follow-up, reactivation campaigns, after-hours voice |

Rationale: sits under Avoca, just above Sameday's floor with a performance option Sameday does not advertise. Performance fees need clean attribution (ESTIMATE risk: disputes), so use CRM-booked jobs only and a cap.

**Pilot (14 days), no revenue guarantee.** Day 0: read-only audit of call logs and lead sources; days 1-14: run text-back and lead response on 1 line at flat pilot fee ($0 to $199 refundable credit); day 14: report leads contacted, conversations, appointments booked, estimated (not guaranteed) value using owner's own close rates. Contract language: no guaranteed results, month-to-month, cancel anytime. Do not market "we guarantee X in revenue."

## 5. Compliance summary (not legal advice; confirm with a TCPA attorney)

- **TCPA one-to-one consent.** The FCC's one-to-one consent rule was vacated by the 11th Circuit; the FCC reinstated the prior "prior express written consent" standard on 29 Aug 2025 ([ActiveProspect](https://activeprospect.com/blog/fcc-one-to-one-consent/), [ComplianceHub](https://compliancehub.wiki/tcpa-2026-consent-revocation-one-to-one-rule-vacated-compliance/)). Confidence MED-HIGH.
- **Revocation rules.** Parts of the 2024 revocation rules took effect April 2025 (per commentary); the "revoke-all" provision was delayed to 31 Jan 2027 by an FCC order of 6 Jan 2026 ([FCC DA 26-12](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-26-12A1.pdf), [CFS Law Monitor](https://www.consumerfinancialserviceslawmonitor.com/2026/01/fcc-further-extends-effective-date-for-tcpa-revoke-all-rule/)). Status after this date not checked; recheck before launch. Design now to honor any STOP/opt-out language across all channels.
- **Quiet hours and frequency.** Federal telemarketing hours 8am-9pm recipient local time (general TCPA rule; not re-verified this pass). Florida and Oklahoma mini-TCPAs: 8am-8pm, max 3 solicitation attempts per 24 hours on the same subject ([Klaviyo FTSA](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4405332994843), [Justia OK 15-775C.1](https://law.justia.com/codes/oklahoma/title-15/section-15-775c-1/), [Manatt](https://www.manatt.com/insights/newsletters/tcpa-connect/oklahomas-mini-tcpa-takes-effect)). FTSA 2023 amendments require STOP reply before suit over continued texts ([Postscript](https://help.postscript.io/en/articles/13563940-compliance-update-florida-messaging)). Washington: I did not verify its commercial-text statute (CEMA); flag for counsel. Simplest policy: apply the strictest rule (8am-8pm, 3 per 24h) nationally.
- **Missed-call text-back nuance (ESTIMATE of legal posture).** A reply to a caller who just called is generally treated as informational/responsive, lower risk than marketing, but any promo content converts it to marketing needing written consent. Dormant-lead reactivation is marketing: require documented consent and honor opt-outs.
- **A2P 10DLC.** Since 1 Feb 2025, unregistered 10DLC traffic is blocked by carriers; brand and campaign must be registered with The Campaign Registry ([TxtImpact](https://www.txtimpact.com/blog/a2p-10dlc-registration-guide), [Quo](https://www.quo.com/blog/what-is-a2p-10dlc/)). Budget time per customer for registration (approval timelines not verified). Recommend toll-free or 10DLC per customer, not a shared number.
- **AI voice.** FCC Declaratory Ruling of 8 Feb 2024: AI-generated voices are "artificial or prerecorded voice" under the TCPA; consent is required for outbound calls; statutory damages $500-$1,500 per call ([FCC](https://www.fcc.gov/document/fcc-makes-ai-generated-voices-robocalls-illegal), [Wiley](https://www.wiley.law/alert-FCC-Extends-Regulatory-Reach-Over-AI-Announces-TCPA-Restrictions-Cover-AI-Generated-Voices-in-Outbound-Calls)). Inbound answering by AI is not the restricted case, but disclose the AI at call start as best practice (some state laws exist; not researched). No outbound AI voice calls without written consent.
- **Call recording.** Two-party/all-party consent states (as of 2026 per [Recording Law](https://www.recordinglaw.com/united-states-recording-laws/)): CA, DE, FL, IL, MD, MA, MT, NV, NH, PA, WA. Play a recording notice on every call. (Some sources list additional states; recheck.)
- **CAN-SPAM.** Applies to commercial email: accurate headers, physical address, working unsubscribe honored within 10 business days (from general knowledge, not re-verified this pass).

## 6. First-customer acquisition ranking (ESTIMATE, limited direct evidence)

1. **Free audit-first outbound to HVAC/plumbing owners** (mystery-call test of their own number after hours, plus LSA/Angi response test): demonstrates the leak with their own data; the audit is our wedge. Evidence: competitor landscape shows nobody leads with an audit (my observation).
2. **Referral/partnership with LSA/PPC agencies and consultants** who feel the pain of wasted spend (Angi/LSA shared-lead complaints: [LeadTruffle](https://www.leadtruffle.co/blog/complete-guide-angi-leads-home-service-contractors-2026/)). Agencies control many accounts.
3. **CRM/FSM ecosystem presence** (Housecall Pro, Jobber, ServiceTitan marketplaces and user communities); these three dominate the space ([FieldPulse](https://www.fieldpulse.com/resources/blog/servicetitan-vs-housecall-pro)).
4. **Trade communities and Facebook groups, contractor coaches** (owners talk about missed calls openly). Not researched with data.
5. **Cold call/SMS outbound**: SMS cold outreach carries TCPA risk; use email/LinkedIn/phone with care.
6. Paid ads: expensive (LSA CPL $39-$162) and we would be marketing the same leak.
