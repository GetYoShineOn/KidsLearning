👋 Hi, grown-ups!

All-Star Math Arena grows with your child. Pick an age on the home screen (3–4, 5–6, 7–8, or 9–10) — each one has its own levels and gets harder as kids progress. Every star is earned by real practice, never empty taps.

Skills across the ages:

  • Counting, adding, taking away
  • Multiplication, division & fractions (older kids)
  • Shapes, colors & color-by-number
  • Letters, beginning sounds, reading & spelling
  • Patterns, comparing numbers, telling time
  • 💰 Money & financial literacy at every age — needs vs wants, coins, saving, making change, budgeting, interest, and how stocks & investing work
  • 🌎 Spanish or French words (colors, numbers, animals, food) with native audio, plus 🗣️ “Say It!” levels where kids hear a word and repeat it out loud

Choose Spanish or French on the home screen. Each age keeps its own progress, so several kids can play. Wrong taps are gentle — kids try again, never get punished.

🔊 Directions are read aloud in a friendly voice so pre-readers can play on their own. Tap any question to hear it again. The speaker button mutes all sound and narration.

“Say It!” practice is encouragement-based: your child hears the word and repeats it during the listening moment (no microphone needed). On iPad, tap Share → “Add to Home Screen” for full-screen, offline play.
